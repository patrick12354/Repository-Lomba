## Description

```
PlantPulse is a reliability decision workspace for petrochemical plants, built by Team Heisenberg for CALIBER 2026, Case 2: Intelligence Manufacturing.

Check the pulse, not just the patch.

This 3-minute demo follows one real incident end to end: compressor KO-3201 tripped on April 29, 2026, with 32 hours offline and US$1.58M of recorded loss. The evidence was split across four files that didn't agree, and after the repair the follow-up fixes were still open. PlantPulse takes that one incident from KPI, to root cause, to a verified action in one workspace.

What you'll see
• Executive overview: 380 incidents across 12 plants and 5 RCA cases, with real and possible losses kept separate
• Problem Tank: all 220 open records ranked by risk and due date, using recorded values only
• Evidence: every number links back to its source row; conflicts get an owner instead of being quietly resolved
• Machine checkup: every reading shown on the part where it is measured
• AI investigator: likely causes ranked, similar past incidents found, a draft report written, and an Evidence Guard that flags any sentence whose number isn't in its cited source
• Actions & verification: every fix gets an owner, a due date and the proof needed to close it; someone other than the owner signs off, and every decision goes into a tamper-evident log
• Data foundation: 16 source files → 6 quality checks → 1 governed data model; 23 of 23 reproduction checks pass

In our internal blind test (RCA hidden), the recorded cause ranked first in 7 of 9 runs and in the top three every time.

PlantPulse only reads plant data and never changes it, keeps a person in every decision, and claims only what it can measure.
```
