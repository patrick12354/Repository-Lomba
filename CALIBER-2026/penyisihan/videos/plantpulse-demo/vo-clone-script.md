# Voice clone recording script (ElevenLabs Instant Voice Clone)

Tone: energetic and excited, like showing a friend something cool you just built. Smile while you talk.
Let your voice go up and down. Short sentences, so you can punch each one.

## Part A (~30 s)

> Okay, let me show you something really cool!
> Imagine a huge factory, full of big machines.
> They work all day, and all night.
> But sometimes, one of them just stops.
> And when that happens? Everything stops too!
> That costs a lot of money. Like, millions!
> So we asked one simple question.
> What if we could see the problem coming?

## Part B (~30 s, recommended)

> And guess what? We built it!
> It's called PlantPulse.
> It shows you every machine, in one place.
> Red means trouble. Green means good.
> It tells you what went wrong, and why.
> And it tells you exactly what to fix next.
> No more guessing. No more digging around.
> Pretty awesome, right? Let's take a look!

## Recording tips

- Stand up if you can. You'll naturally sound more alive.
- Smile while you read. It really changes the sound.
- Exaggerate a little more than feels normal. On a recording it comes out just right.
- Quiet room, no fan/AC. Phone or mic ~15–20 cm from your mouth.
- Keep the same distance the whole time. Don't shout into the mic.
- If you stumble, pause and re-read the line; cut the mistake later.
- No music, no heavy noise reduction or effects. Export WAV or high-bitrate MP3.
